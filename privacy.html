FRMOIN Miner Privacy

FRMOIN Miner may process Telegram-provided Mini App data such as Telegram user ID, display name, username, and profile photo when supplied by Telegram.

The service may also store game data such as points, mining level, taps, referral relationships, daily reward state, and an optional connected TON wallet address.

This data is used to authenticate users, operate the game, prevent abuse, calculate rewards, and provide referral and wallet features.

Never send private keys or seed phrases to FRMOIN Miner. Wallet connection should be performed through TON Connect.

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, use the official FRMOIN support channel linked from the Mini App.
