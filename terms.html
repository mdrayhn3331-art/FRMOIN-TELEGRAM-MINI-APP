FRMOIN Miner Terms

FRMOIN Miner is a Telegram Mini App that provides an in-app points and mining-style game experience.

Points shown in the app are virtual game points unless a separate official announcement states otherwise. They are not a promise of cryptocurrency, cash, profit, or guaranteed value.

Users must not exploit bugs, automate taps, manipulate requests, or attempt to access another user's account or balance.

Wallet connection is optional. Users are responsible for reviewing and approving any wallet transaction before signing it.

We may change game rules, rewards, limits, or features to maintain security and fair use.

Contact: use the official FRMOIN support channel linked from the Mini App.
